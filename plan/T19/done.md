Delivered: new leaf crate `scull-image` (RGBA8 `Image` behind `Arc` with a generation, `Placement` kept apart from images, `ImageStore` with a byte quota and LRU eviction of unplaced images first) and three streaming decoders with size caps checked before allocation: iTerm2 `File=` (base64, PNG via `png`, JPEG via `zune-jpeg`), sixel, and the kitty graphics protocol (chunked uploads, formats 24/32/100, zlib, t/T/q/p/d actions, image numbers, quiet levels, replies; written from the spec, no kitty code). `scull-term` routes DCS `q`, OSC 1337 and APC `G` into them, moves the cursor per protocol, sends kitty replies through the capped reply queue, keeps placements on absolute lines, prunes evicted ones, re-anchors them on reflow through T11 `TrackPoint`s, keeps a separate image layer per screen (cleared when the alt screen is left), and handles ED 2 and RIS. The frame carries placements sorted by z and damage covers rows under a changed placement. `scull-ffi` ABI 0.2 adds refcounted `tt_image` (`retain`, `release`, `pixels`) and `tt_placement` in the frame view; `tt_term_resize` pixel sizes set the cell size. The OSC cap in scull-parser rose to 16 MiB for iTerm2 images and shrinks back to 64 KiB after. Tests: 60 in scull-image, 32 image golden tests through `Terminal::feed`, the T9 frame property test extended with random images, FFI tests, and the two-thread C test reading pixels under ASan+UBSan and TSan. Fuzz targets `iterm`, `sixel`, `kitty` and `term_images` ran 1.3–2.9M inputs each without crashes; the kitty target found a base64 slicing bug, fixed with a regression test. T19.4 was split into T19.4a and T19.4b to fit the budget.

Left out: region scrolls (DECSTBM) do not move images, text written over an image does not erase it, auto-sized kitty placements can stretch by up to one cell, no iTerm2 multipart or animation, no kitty Unicode placeholders, animation or relative placements, and no FFI setters for the sixel background or the cell size apart from `tt_term_resize`.
