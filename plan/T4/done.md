Delivered: own Williams-table state machine behind a SWAR bulk scanner (`vtparse` buffers APC in an unbounded Vec and parses byte by byte; `vte` drops APC). `Parser::feed` hands printable UTF-8 runs to `Handler::print` in one piece, keeps a split UTF-8 character across calls, streams DCS and APC payloads as input slices, and keeps OSC in one reused buffer. Caps: 32 params, 2 intermediates, OSC 1 MiB, DCS 16 MiB, APC 4 MiB, each with an overflow test. 46 tests including a chunking-invariance property. Fuzz target `parser`: 5,081,125 runs in 121 s, no crashes after an underflow fix. divan bench against vte 0.15 on 1 MiB: plain ASCII 3.05 vs 1.22 GB/s (2.5x), UTF-8 1.31 vs 0.80 GB/s (1.6x), SGR-heavy at parity. Left open: the bench is parser-level, not yet wired into the T3 harness; no flush for a UTF-8 character held when the PTY closes; a UTF-8 C1 ST (U+009C) does not end a string; about 685 lines of non-test code, over the 500-line budget.
